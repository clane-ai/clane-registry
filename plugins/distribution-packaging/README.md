# Distribution & packaging

Orders, invoices and the inbox, set up for a distributor.

One Add in Clane's **Add an agent** window brings:

- three ready-made agents: **Order Desk**, **Invoice Clerk** and **Inbox Assistant**
  (the same agents as on their own; nothing here is a copy of them);
- three Workflows, added as drafts that are switched off until you switch one on:
  **Orders from email**, **PO to order form** and **Invoices by your rules**.

The Workflow folders under `clane/workflows/` are copies of the examples in Clane's
Workflow authoring skill.
